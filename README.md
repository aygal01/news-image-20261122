One image that sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026, designed like a newspaper front page: a prominent date masthead, the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya as the hero story, and captioned panels covering the tanker strikes near the Strait of Hormuz as Iran unveiled its underground "missile city", the sea-drone sinking of a Russian warship in Benghazi port, Russian strikes on Kyiv flats and Zelenskyy's hometown, the World Court's ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the Democrats winning the US House with their largest majority since 1992, and Britain and the EU striking deals to rebuild post-Brexit ties — plus a ticker with the West Bank settler arson attacks and the Nigeria funeral-boat capsizing that killed at least 24. The finished image ships in the repo as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output directory and verifies the copy (sha256, 1 MiB cap, JPEG/PNG magic bytes), while `build_image.py` documents exactly how the composite was built from the panel art in `assets/`.
